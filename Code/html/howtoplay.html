How to Play

Everything you need to know to become a TypeRush Grand Line champion.

⚓ Game Overview

TypeRush is a 3-level pirate-themed typing challenge. Each level tests a different skill: image recognition, paragraph typing, and audio dictation. You have 45 seconds and 5 lives per level to earn the highest score possible.

🗺️ Step-by-Step Guide

01

Create an Account

Register with your personal details and username. Logging in allows your scores, profile stats, and leaderboard results to be saved.

02

Choose a Level

Go to the Game page and choose a voyage. During testing, all levels may be unlocked so each feature can be checked easily.

03

Start the Round

Click Start. The timer begins, the input box activates, and the level challenge appears on screen.

04

Type Accurately

Type the image word, paragraph, or audio transcript as accurately as possible. Correct typing improves your score.

05

Protect Your Lives

You have 5 lives per level. Each mistake reduces your score, and losing all lives ends the session immediately.

06

Check Your Result

At the end of a level, your WPM, accuracy, mistakes, bonus, score, and rank are displayed on the result screen.

🎮 The Three Levels

1

Level 1 — Image Typing

A Canva-made image appears on screen. Type the word represented by the image. This level tests recognition, spelling, and speed.

2

Level 2 — Paragraph Race

Type the paragraph as quickly and accurately as possible. Correct letters appear green, while wrong letters appear red.

3

Level 3 — Audio Dictation

Listen to the recording and type what you hear. This level tests listening, memory, focus, speed, and accuracy.

📊 Stats Explained

⏱

Time

Each level lasts 45 seconds. When the timer reaches zero, the session ends and your result is shown.

⚡

WPM

Words Per Minute shows your typing speed. It updates while you play and contributes to your final result.

🎯

Accuracy

Accuracy shows how many characters you typed correctly. Higher accuracy gives a stronger score.

💰

Score

Your score is based on correct typing, WPM, accuracy, streak bonuses, and mistake penalties.

❤️

Lives

You have 5 lives per level. Mistakes reduce your lives and score. If all lives are lost, the session ends.

🔥

Streak Bonus

A streak rewards consistent correct typing. The better your streak, the more bonus points you can earn.

🏆 Leaderboard

The leaderboard displays the best player scores. Scores are saved using localStorage, so registered users can compete for the highest bounty.

🎖️ Result Screen

After each level, the result screen shows your final WPM, accuracy, score, mistakes, bonus, time taken, and rank. You can retry, go back to the menu, or continue to the next challenge.

💡 Pro Tips

🔑

Balance Speed and Accuracy

Typing fast is useful, but too many mistakes will reduce your score. Aim for smooth and accurate typing.

👀

Read Ahead

In Level 2, try to read the next word while typing the current one. This helps improve speed.

🎧

Listen Carefully

In Level 3, focus on the recording before typing. Replay the audio if needed during practice.

🔄

Retry Often

Use Retry to practise each level. Improving your score helps you climb the leaderboard.
